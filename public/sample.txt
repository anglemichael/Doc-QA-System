CAMPUS GARDEN PROJECT

The campus garden project is coordinated by Asha Rao. The garden is located in the North Courtyard beside the library.

The project begins on 12 March 2026. Student volunteers will plant herbs and native flowers. The first planting session starts at 9:30 AM.

The project budget is 2,500 rupees. The budget covers seeds, soil, and reusable gardening tools. The facilities team will provide watering cans.

The project aims to create a quiet outdoor study area and support local pollinators. Volunteers should bring a reusable water bottle and wear comfortable shoes.
